# Tell us about one moment

What were you trying to do? What happened? What did you expect instead?
If it helped, tell us what became easier. If you stopped, tell us where.

[Send feedback on GitHub](https://github.com/yifanh233/extended-mind/issues/new?title=%5BPilot%20feedback%5D&body=What%20I%20tried%3A%0A%0AWhat%20happened%3A%0A%0AWhat%20I%20expected%3A%0A%0AAgent%20and%20version%20%28optional%29%3A%0A)
or reply privately to the person who invited you. The link works without a
custom form; a guided form is included in this candidate for the next publish.

GitHub feedback is public. Include only an excerpt you are comfortable sharing,
not your private project, credentials or full session log. Nothing is uploaded
automatically. Agent/version, session update ID and a workaround are optional.

For the assistant: save volunteered feedback in the owning scope with the
user's words, source/date and sharing permission. Label your interpretation
separately. Never submit it externally without authorization.
